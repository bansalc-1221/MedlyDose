Crash the app

Kills the app process instantly. Tests the watchdog restart.

Freeze the app

The app stays alive but stops answering. Tests how the watchdog spots a frozen app.

SMS service

Takes the reminder service offline for 12 seconds. It comes back by itself, like a real outage. Tests the circuit breaker: it stops calling the dead service, then tests it again and resumes.

Internet connection

Cuts the link to the caregiver cloud. Tests the offline queue. Take a dose on Today while it's cut, then restore it.

Corrupt the database

Slips one deliberately broken record (blank name, dose -5, time 25:99) into the database, like bad data from a faulty import. It is never a real medicine and is never shown in your list. The app finds it within about 3 seconds and sets it aside in quarantine. Watch the Health page.

Flood the app

One client sends hundreds of requests a second for 8 seconds, like results-day traffic. Tests load shedding: the flood is refused, everyone else is served normally.

Wipe all data (can't be healed)

Deletes every medicine and all history. The app notices the loss and raises the alarm, but there is no backup, so it cannot get the data back. This is the limit we are honest about. It really deletes your data.
