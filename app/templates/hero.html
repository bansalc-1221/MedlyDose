MedlyDose

Never miss a dose.

MedlyDose keeps your medicine schedule, reminds you when each dose is due, and shares what you've taken with the person looking after you. If part of it breaks, it fixes itself.

Get started

Your medicines stay on this computer.

Reminders that arrive on time

Set a bell before a dose is due. When the time comes you get an alert and a text message, and the Taken button appears.

A calendar of every day

See what you took, what you missed and what's still to come, one day at a time.

Shared with your caregiver

Each dose you take is passed on. If the internet drops, it's saved on this device and sent when it's back.

Fixes itself when it breaks

If the app crashes, freezes or a service stops answering, it notices and recovers without anyone restarting it.

A missed dose can hurt someone, so MedlyDose watches itself.

Four protections run all the time. Break things on purpose from the Chaos page and watch each one recover, with the times written down.
